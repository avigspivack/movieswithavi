One of the all-time great American films, and one of only three movies ever to sweep the Oscars w Best picture, director, actor and actress. I just rewatched it for the nth time, and wow, it still holds up. It’s funny that there is no internet and no cellphones, and of course, these add to the raw texture of the film, which is filled with tight close-ups and first-person shots that embed us in Clarice’s experience. Jodie Foster and Hopkins are magnetic together, Foster especially, surrounded by men and still holding her own. It’s quite nearly perfect, and it’s also a great example of why you should pretty much never make sequels (or prequels). The OG is always the best.
