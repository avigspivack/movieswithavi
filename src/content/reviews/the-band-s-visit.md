The late Israeli actress Ronit Elkabetz seems like she was born to act; there is something magnetic about her. We first saw her in the intense movie “Gett.” She is amazing to watch in this film - she seems to be trapped in a desolate Israeli town but her spirit is strong and full of light. And her chemistry with another Israeli titan, Sasson Gabai (who you will recognize from the excellent series “Tehran”) is awkwardly wonderful and true.  There are some fantastic comedic moments here, played perfectly by this unexpected collection of a cast. In the end we are left nodding in human satisfaction. Just wait for the roller-skating scenes. Magic.
