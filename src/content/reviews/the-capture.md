We stumbled into this one on Amazon Prime initially, and it’s impossible not to be sucked in. Season one is electric, with a young and raw Callum Turner starring alongside the perfectly cast Holliday Grainger as Rachel Carey. The premise centers around the manipulation of video imagery and extends into deepfaking in the second and third seasons. You will be riveted and highly entertained, as the creators keep you guessing and off balance in the most delicious ways. Season four is unconfirmed, but I really hope they come through with it - so much great acting and modern material that we could keep going for years.
