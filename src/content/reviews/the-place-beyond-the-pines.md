Let’s just say that this was not the movie I thought it would be, and that’s generally a good thing. You watch the trailer and then it starts and it feels like a version of “Drive” on a motorcycle set in upstate New York. But it takes a wildly unexpected turn, which I won’t reveal here. And then we find ourselves in a new storyline and then another, and then they all come together. It’s far from perfect, but it’s bold and resonant. The camera stays in tight, the tension is high and uneasy. You feel the unease, the desire to escape and outrun. And we are immersed in the world of men - fathers, sons, and duty. None of which is simple or clear.
