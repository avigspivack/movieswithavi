Let’s just say that this was not the movie I thought it would be, and that’s generally a good thing. You watch the trailer and then it starts - a tattooed Gosling preparing to perform, and it feels like a version of “Drive” on a motorcycle set in upstate New York. But it takes a wildly unexpected turn, which I won’t reveal here. And then we find ourselves in a new storyline and then another, and then they eventually come together. It’s far from perfect, but it’s bold and resonant. The camera stays in tight, the subtle lighting reflects the characters’ unease, and the tension is high. You feel the anxiety, the desire to escape and outrun. Younger Gosling and Cooper are both excellent, in ways you might not expect. And we are immersed in the world of men - fathers, sons, and duty to family. None of which is simple or clear.
