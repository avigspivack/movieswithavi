In case you don’t know him, Egon Schiele was an Austrian painter whose style is very distinct - he was a mentee of Klimt and you’d know one of his paintings as soon as you see it. He also only lived to 28, dying during the Spanish flu pandemic of 1918. The story told in this film is based on the true journey of one of his paintings from 1914 - "Wilted Sunflowers (Autumn Sun II)" (also known as Sonnenblumen). It’s a beautiful and small story, told with some somber tones, and we are reminded of the horrific history of the Nazis and the moral quandaries surrounding the painting, as well as the layers of complexity that are interwoven throughout all of our personal relationships - both to others and ourselves.
