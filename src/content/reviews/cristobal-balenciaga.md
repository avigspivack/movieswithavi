This one is so good we are re-watching it! It helps if you are semi interested in fashion and history, but even if you’re not, the narrative structure and acting in this series is so amazing, you’d be missing out. Spanish actor Alberto San Juan is simply incredible, playing the lead across many decades of Balenciaga’s life. His performance alone makes it worthwhile, and you should stay for the abundance of designs and the stunning texture of the story and its cinematography.
