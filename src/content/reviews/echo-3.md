This is a gripping series on Apple that somehow flew under the radar - serious production with a real cinematic texture to it; each episode unfolds like a mini movie. It’s based on the Israeli book and show called “When Heroes Fly,” and it’s clear that creator / adapter Mark Boal took real pride in the casting and accuracy. You know she’s going to get kidnapped and you assume she will be rescued, but they keep you guessing and that’s part of the fun. A serious, ponderous, and very well-paced piece of work. Definitely worth a binge.
