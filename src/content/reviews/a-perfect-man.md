The duo of French writer / director Yann Gozlan and actor Pierre Niney has become one to watch - we saw their films out of order; this one is from 2015, and their other, “Black Box,” is from 2021. Both share the same magnetism to Niney, an immediate attachment to him, and he plays the Hitchcockian terror quite well. He is so slender that you’d think a strong ocean breeze would blow him over, but his presence is immense, and he glides through the film with fluidity, and even a believability. This one is wrapped in deep amorality, and even though I was able to predict what would happen, it was beautiful, enthralling, and so very French.
