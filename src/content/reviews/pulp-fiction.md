It’s funny I’ve waited this long to write this one since I saw “Pulp Fiction” at least three times in the theater when it came out in 1994. I didn’t realize how much I loved movies yet, but it was definitely the start of something - Tarantino changed what movies could be. A film could be people talking, and talking and acting like the coolest people you’ve ever met. It didn’t matter if normal folks don’t actually talk like that - in fact, that was the whole point. And the story didn’t need to be linear. He flipped it all, and this awesome originality is the thing to celebrate. What a joy. We may never get another one like it.
