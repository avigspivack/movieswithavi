My disclaimer is that I saw this on the plane and now I will probably watch it again. Give me anything from “Hell or High Water” director David Mackenzie and I’m in, don’t even need to ask me twice. In fact I am shocked I didn’t know about this one until I was scrolling the catalog on our recent flight home. I think it didn’t do well because of the title, not kidding - it’s too clever for its own good. Just go and watch this one because it’s got all the things you want - like all his movies (including the very good “Relay”), we are immediately thrust into the action, the pace is excellent, always moving, and we are continually guessing what will happen next because we actually don’t know, and we are sufficiently fooled multiple times. Say what you will about the ending; my bar wasn’t so high for this film because it doesn’t try to be more than what it is - compared to HOHW, which has some morality and tenderness. This one is straight action and proud of it.
