On a tight $7m budget, this is a fantastic film with real originality and excellent performances (including the piano pieces). Canadian director Daniel Roher is an auteur to watch - he is developing a real distinct style and pacing, and he extracts superb acting from everyone, but especially Leo Woodall in the lead (hard to believe he’s British!). We first saw him in the short series “Prime Target,” and now it looks like he’s everywhere; he has a distinct look, and he plays the flawed Nikki with an admirable quietness. You can somehow tell the film was made by a young director, reminded me slightly of Ben Younger’s “Boiler Room,” specifically in how he portrayed NYC, and a young man finding his way in life and love. The piano is the ever-present secondary character, just waiting to be played.
