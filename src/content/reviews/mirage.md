Spanish auteur Orio Paulo is right up there for me as the creator of some of favorites - the slick and surprising “The Invisible Guest,” the shocking “The Body,” and the excellent but dark Harlan Coben adaptation “The Innocent” (starring the hunky Mario Casas). He loves to play with time and mystery and fate, where the past can define us and come back to haunt us - revenge and redemption are key themes. “Mirage” is in the same vein, and it delivers. The texture is dark and the narrative is a total puzzle; you also need to pay close attention as clues are revealed throughout so you can piece the story together. Deliciously enjoyable.
