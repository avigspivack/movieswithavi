We saw this French gem 25 years ago when it came out (probably in the theater!), and we just rewatched it because it’s that good and demanded to be seen again. It totally holds up, having aged like a fine wine. You don’t know where director Dominik Moll is taking you, and then you can’t believe what transpires; it’s an utterly shocking and delightful experience, and I highly recommend it. It’s kinda like a superior, French version of “The Gift.” And the homage to Hitchcock’s “Psycho” is both overt and so sly, you can’t help but smirk.
