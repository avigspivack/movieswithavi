This is another beautiful film about a family in the South (Kentucky) from the same writer/director who created "Junebug" (the remarkably astute Angus MacLachlan). It is a small huge movie because it manages to encompass every dynamic - spouses, young kids, adult children, and in laws. It is staggering in its simplicity and universality, and I suspect - like me - you will say it’s the best you’ve ever seen David Strathairn, one of our great actors who has quietly but assuredly built a serious resume mostly in the background. A rare gift of a film, and one that I will likely watch again when I’m in the mood for depth and honesty. As it should be.
