While this movie won’t dazzle you or make you go all introspective, it will deliver a ton of fun while in Nice (always a nice place to be), and looking at some beautiful people on the run. The always charming Yvan Attal has wonderful chemistry with the stunning Sophie Marceau, and we are mostly just happy to be hanging out with them while the cops and mafia chase Zimmer. In typical French thriller fashion, the story is very well-paced and clocks in at a tidy 89 minutes - perfect for an evening watch when you just want some delightful entertainment.
