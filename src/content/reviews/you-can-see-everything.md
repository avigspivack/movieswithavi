Elizabeth Holmes’ eyes are unusual, and she can seemingly hold a stare for longer than seems normal or comfortable. It’s no wonder they decided to put one of these bright blue eyes at the center of the movie poster, and if you look closely, you’ll also see the blurred image of comedian and creator Nathan Fielder looking on in the background; also not an accident. This is, quite simply, an extraordinary work of art, a documentary but not really a documentary, and you absolutely must see it at least once (I am ready to see it again three days later). I’m not going to reveal anything here, only to say that it’s brilliantly filmed and told, and despite all the hype - it actually lives up to and exceeds anything I expected going in. Even if you don’t know the story of Theranos, you will be pulled in to Holmes’ life and question everything you do or don’t believe about what is true.
