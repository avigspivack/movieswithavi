It’s always tough when the bar is high before I watch something, because of course the expectation is there and anything less is then underwhelming. PHM (abbreviating the title to ease the burden of my fingers) suffered such a fate - I had read too many positive reviews and seen too many glowing notes and videos about this movie, so unfortunately it was a tad disappointing. I mean, don’t get me wrong, I would watch Ryan and Sandra Huller all day in pretty much anything, but somehow this film left me wanting a little more substance vs a cute outer space relationship with a rock named Rocky. It’s definitely a fun ride and worth the 2.5 hours, ideally on a very big screen, just know you’re choosing the fun and that’s all. And sometimes that’s all you need.
