A reminder to my readers that there are very few movies that I dislike - I always find the good in everything I choose to watch (and I am quite choosy). This isn’t a great film, but there are many great aspects about it: It’s fun to watch Turturro at home in NYC, riding the subway all over, looking for his next score; Buscemi and a surprise cameo at the end are nice touches; the pace moves well but doesn’t rush, and the violence and danger is hinted but it never devolves (as many other movies tend to do). It won’t change your life but you will likely appreciate these notes, and you will hopefully leave the theatre with a sense of satisfaction.
