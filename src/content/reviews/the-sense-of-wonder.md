I will start with a confession: I will watch anything that includes Virginie Efira; she is on my personal Mount Rushmore of French actresses even though she’s technically Belgian (the peak also includes Isabel Huppert, Laure Calamy, and Charlotte Gainsbourg - topic for a different time). And this is a warm and wise and lustrously textured movie, filled with color everywhere, tasty treats, and a pull to transport you to the little Provence town where it takes place. As is often the case, even though you know how it will end, the experience is so worthwhile that you don’t even care.
