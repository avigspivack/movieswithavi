I’ve been admiring Ludivine Sagnier for more than 20 years - I encountered her first in Francois Ozon’s sexy thriller “Swimming Pool” alongside the epic Charlotte Rampling. This movie is seven years later, and you can see how she’s matured, able to hold her own as the lead and appearing in nearly every scene. This is a wonderful watch because it’s like the film has a smirk on the whole time, and it makes you question what’s happening just enough to be sucked in, and then wondering how the third act will unfold. Kristin Scott Thomas plays the devious boss with her trademark wit, and the two women are sparks from the very first scene. But it’s the transformation we observe throughout that’s so appealing here - gradually and then suddenly.
